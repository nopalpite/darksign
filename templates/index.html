Media library

Raspberry Pi 3: convert your videos on your computer before uploading them. The player can do it, but on this model the conversion is slow and saturates the Pi, to the point of disturbing playback. A video already in the right format is stored as is, instantly.

With HandBrake (free, with an interface)
  1. Install HandBrake (Windows, macOS, Linux) and open your video.
  2. Preset: General › Fast 1080p30. It produces H.264 in MP4 with AAC sound, 1080p and 30 frames/s at most: the player format.
  3. Summary tab: MP4 format, tick Web Optimized.
  4. Start Encode, then upload the .mp4 file you get.
